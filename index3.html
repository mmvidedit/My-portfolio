FAQ

Common Questions

How fast is turnaround? +
Most short-form edits are delivered within 2–4 days, depending on volume and complexity. Longer or multi-camera projects are usually quoted individually.
How many revisions do I get? +
Every project includes revision rounds until you're happy with the final cut. Small tweaks (timing, captions, music) are always included at no extra cost.
What footage do you need from me? +
Raw clips in the highest quality you have, plus any brief, reference links, or brand guidelines. If you're not sure what to send, I'll walk you through it.
Do you handle captions and sound design? +
Yes — captions, sound design, music, and color grading are all part of the standard edit, not add-ons.
Can we work together on a monthly basis? +
Yes, monthly plans are available for ongoing content needs, with a discounted rate per video — see the pricing section above for details.
